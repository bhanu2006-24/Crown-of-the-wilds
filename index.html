The Core Premise

You play as Princess Aurelia. When Lord Malakor shatters the kingdom's sacred Aegis Crystal, the peaceful Aura Spirits become corrupted. Disguised in traveling clothes, you must explore the realm, cleanse the spirits to make them companions, recover the 3 lost crystal fragments, and reclaim your throne!

Active Mechanics in Playable Prologue

1. Castle Town: Speak with Scholar Alistair, Chancellor Vance, and confront Lord Malakor in the Sanctum.
2. The Shattering: Watch the crystal break and escape into the secret sewers.
3. The Royal Sewers: Explore the undercroft, fight the Corrupted Fox Spirit, and use Cleanse to recruit Pyropaw as your loyal companion!

Controls

• Movement: WASD, Arrow Keys, or Click/Tap anywhere on map.
• Action / Interact: Space, Enter, Z, or Click.
• Pause Menu: Esc, X, or top-right MENU button.
• Fullscreen: F key or top-right Fullscreen button.
